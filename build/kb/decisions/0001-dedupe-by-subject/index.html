0001 · Duplicates share a subject line

owner rune accepted reviewed 2024-06-11 · 871 days ago · stale

Nobody has re-read this page in 871 days. Check it against the live views before you rely on it.

Date: 11 June 2024 · Deciders: Rune (product), support desk

Context

Reports arrive faster than the desk can read them. During incidents the same problem comes in hundreds of times, and engineering asks for one ticket per issue.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when their subject lines match after lowercasing and stripping punctuation. The desk merges on subject alone; nobody has to read the body.

Consequences

  • Fast: one person can triage an incident morning.
  • Reports with different subjects about the same bug end up in separate tickets. Accepted; engineering merges them later.
