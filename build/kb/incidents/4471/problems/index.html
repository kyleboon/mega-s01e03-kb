How a report is placed

  1. One id, one report. The export repeats some rows; they are counted once.
  2. One-day move. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) is not a flip: the day and the month stay where they are and the date shifts by a day, or the same meeting lands on different days for people in different places. That points at time zones, not at the parser. It has its own ticket, and stays counted under the cause its tenant gives it, as the desk counts.
  3. hint. A report that came in through the booking form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads the date day first. People type what the hint says, so their dates come out flipped even with a fixed parser.
  4. parser. Every other report from a day-first tenant (the one-day moves included): a date the shared parser read month first.

The intake tag is not used. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant for the count shown under the table.
